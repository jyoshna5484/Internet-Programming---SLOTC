Common Animal Diseases

🐄 Foot and Mouth Disease

Symptoms: Fever, mouth sores and difficulty walking.

Care: Veterinary examination and isolation.

🐕 Rabies

Symptoms: Behavioral changes and excessive salivation.

Care: Immediate veterinary attention.

🐐 PPR Disease

Symptoms: Fever, discharge and breathing problems.

Care: Consult a veterinary doctor immediately.

🐄 Mastitis

Symptoms: Swelling and pain in the udder.

Care: Veterinary examination and treatment.
