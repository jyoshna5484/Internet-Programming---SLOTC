About Journal

The International Journal of Scientific and Social Excellence (IJSSE) is a multidisciplinary academic journal dedicated to publishing quality research papers in science, technology, engineering, management and social sciences.

Mission

To encourage research, innovation and knowledge sharing among researchers, students and academic professionals.
